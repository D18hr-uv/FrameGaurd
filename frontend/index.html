The Class Activation Map (Grad-CAM) visualizes the decision-making process of our deep neural network.

Red and yellow regions highlight the specific facial features and localized artifacts the AI heavily focused on to classify the image. In deepfakes, these high-impact areas often pinpoint manipulation artifacts such as blending boundaries, unnatural warping, or structural inconsistencies around the eyes, nose, and mouth.

Blue and green regions represent areas the model deemed structurally normal and unmanipulated.
